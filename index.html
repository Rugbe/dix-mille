Who’s playing?

    • Single 1 = 100, single 5 = 50. Three of a kind = face × 100 (three 1s = 1 000), each extra die doubles it.
    • Straight of five = 500, straight of six = 1 000, three pairs = 500.
    • Hand full (every die scores): you must throw again.
    • 500 in one turn to enter the game. Reach exactly 10 000 to win; going over scores nothing.
    • When a player stops, the next one can take over their points and leftover dice.
    • Once in the game, no score = a cross. Three in a row = −1 000.
